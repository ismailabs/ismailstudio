# Requirements and acceptance contract

## Product
Brand: **Ismail Studio**. Independent Malaysian frontend website developer targeting small Malaysian businesses, English single-page marketing/portfolio website. Purpose: convert qualified visitors from organic search, referrals, social and direct outreach to legitimate project enquiries. Design: premium editorial, memorable but readable, high craft and clarity, responsive, professional and trustworthy. Site is not a SaaS app, creative-only playground, multi-page agency site, CMS, client portal, online shop or internal dashboard.

## Commercial offer
- Starter: **From RM599**, **one page**, one revision round, template-informed design.
- Business: **From RM1,299**, **up to 5 pages**, two revision rounds, more custom reusable layouts.
- Premium: **From RM2,999**, **up to 12 pages**, two revision rounds, more extensive reusable layouts.
- Website responsive design, basic on-page SEO and a **static contact-action capability** in the delivery scope, not functional backend. Client sites may have simple contact forms provided a separately agreed form service; not included by implication.
- Copy/design complexity, licenses, domain purchase, recurring third-party fees, ongoing maintenance, content supply, extra requests and timelines to be confirmed per quote. No unlimited sections, pages, design concepts, maintenance, revisions or results. Clarify scope before contractual use.

## Contact
- Primary: accessible quotation request form, preselect package on CTA, managed provider, notification to `contact@ismailstudio.my`, receipt acknowledgment if supported, clear success/failure and data-minimizing privacy explanation.
- Secondary: clickable business email `mailto:contact@ismailstudio.my`.
- Personal WhatsApp number is private; NO public display, tracking or embed. WhatsApp feature can remain as disabled future configuration and must respect account eligibility.
- Zoho mailbox send/receive functionality was user-confirmed; real integration/deliverability must nevertheless be tested.

## Geographic / legal representations
Country: Malaysia nationwide. No invented office/address, local map listing, SSM registration, registered company, testimonials, team size, sales figures or verified conversion increases. Adult/guardian and qualified advice needed for lawful paid commercial arrangements while operator is under 18; do not publicize age. Public site must not imply payment acceptance or a registered company. This does NOT prohibit a truthful website marketing portfolio.

## Visual priorities
1. Clear hero + logo + signature visual; 2. Project previews; 3. Three comprehensible pricing offers; 4. usable enquiry form; 5. editorial polish; 6. subtle scroll typography animation. Deliver bespoke art direction and tangible differences from generic agency templates; no particle backgrounds, carousel clutter or meaningless 3D.

## Integrations/operating costs
Static Cloudflare hosting; managed form service preferably free/low-cost. No self-hosted application backend, database, auth, CRM or automatic outreach. Do not commit to paid plan or connect new services under someone else's eligibility without an authorized, eligible account. Track conversion through provider confirmation plus privacy-conscious aggregated instrumentation if legitimately available; don't represent simple link-click counts as qualified leads.
