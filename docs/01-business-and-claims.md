# Business context, buyers, constraints, messaging evidence

## Buyer problems and hypotheses
The best-fit prospect is a Malaysian small business whose customers cannot quickly discover services/prices/working areas, evaluate examples, or find a useful contact route. These are **hypotheses to validate with owners**, not asserted lost revenue. Secondary opportunities: mobile-friction repair and a dedicated campaign landing page. Relevant segments: car detailing/tinting, home services, event photographers, interior renovation, tuition centres, catering, cafés/restaurants. Restaurant and café demos are useful but not proof those sectors need websites. Qualified prospects need an actual information/enquiry problem and willingness to pay—not simply absence of a website.

## Honest differentiation
Independent developer; direct communication; explicit scope and pricing; business-first information structure; fast, mobile-friendly frontend; practical SEO basics; AI-assisted implementation with personal review and tests. Avoid overstatement: inexperienced is not equivalent to low quality, and mockups are not verified client outcomes. No invented deadline guarantee, free hosting forever, registered business status, awards, number of clients or time in industry.

## Sales conversation / inquiry qualification
Identify business type, services, target audience, desired pages, main issue to fix, existing domain/site, supplied content/images, planned go-live expectations, expected budget and contact email. After receiving inquiry: manual response, scope discussion, quote, appropriate eligible contracting arrangement, written scope, terms, revision/approval schedule and records; no automatic contract or payment.

## Commercial operating guardrails
Use quotation/scope of work, clear deliverables/exclusions, documented changes, fair payment terms and written approvals. Domain, hosting, maintenance, licensed assets and third-party software costs require explicit agreement. No promised SEO rank, revenue or lead growth. Before taking paid work, resolve relevant Malaysian business-registration/contracting rules with parent/guardian and suitable professional guidance. Do not place adult's identity on contracts as a token workaround. Preserve correspondence and issue resolution records.

## Ethical conversion
No fake counters, fabricated urgency, dark patterns, deceptive trust badges or made-up customer reviews. Emphasize proven project demos and transparent processes. The project's metric is **genuine qualified enquiries**; site traffic and CTA clicks are leading signals but not proof of revenue.
