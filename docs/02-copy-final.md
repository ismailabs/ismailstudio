## 08 FAQ
**Can I update my website later?** **Yes. Updates can be arranged through manual code changes or a separately agreed maintenance service. A content management system is not included in these packages.**
**Are domain and hosting included?** **Domain registration and recurring third-party costs are separate unless the written quote says otherwise. Hosting setup can be discussed based on your project.**
**Will my website appear on Google?** **Basic on-page SEO preparation is included, but search engines decide when to index pages and how to rank them. Rankings aren't guaranteed.**
**Do you build online stores or booking systems?** **The current packages focus on frontend websites. Payment checkout, databases and fully operational booking systems are not included.**
**How many revisions are included?** **Starter includes one revision round. Business and Premium each include two, for feedback within the agreed project scope.**
**What do I need to provide?** **Usually your business details, services, preferred content, brand materials or images, and timely feedback. We can clarify the list before starting.**
