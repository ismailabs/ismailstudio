# Brand and art-direction brief — approved

## Purpose and tone

Ismail Studio is an independent Malaysian developer practice. It solves information, communication and presentation problems by making practical, distinctive web experiences. Speak directly in the first person where appropriate; show genuine work rather than grandiose claims. Keep a credible path from admiration → project evidence → understandable offer → enquiry.

Audience A: Malaysian independent/small businesses that want a clear and credible website. Audience B: creative professionals assessing front-end capability. Do not let experimental motion obscure price, contact or service limits.

## Integrated direction: 01 + 02 + 03

- **01 Cinematic immersion:** near-black background, controlled atmospheric depth and selective light, warm/cool contrast, immersive first screen and dramatic transitions.
- **02 Bold contemporary:** imposing typographic voice, aggressive but legible scale, sharp editorial asymmetry, vivid orange for action/identity, high-contrast changes of section tone, unusually intentional project layout.
- **03 Experimental digital:** purpose-built interactive studio symbol / interface, layered and spatial project transitions, engineered detail and pointer/scroll interactions with low-motion equivalents.

A poor implementation looks like a conventional split-column SaaS landing page, 3 equal pricing cards lifted from a template, random chrome 3D blob, six indistinguishable grid sections, generic "innovative solutions" copy, or only a recolor of V1. An equally poor implementation is a slow, inaccessible art experiment that leaves a real business owner unsure what is offered.

## Signature identity thesis

**Problem → interpretation → purposeful digital solution** expressed as a bespoke *IS / portal / framed system* glyph: angular planes or bracketed surfaces reveal or position truthful project screenshots as the viewer interacts. Geometry relates to the wordmark and layout grid. Explore SVG + CSS/Canvas as a progressive enhancement, with static LCP-safe raster/inline fallback. This is a recommended engineering translation of the mockup visual, not a mandate for resource-heavy WebGL.

The glass-sculpture concept is **only a mood cue**. Code should own layout, copy, and actual interactions. Avoid reusing it as the primary final hero unless its design rationale, originality and performance are demonstrated.

## Narrative and voice

1. Recognize a business or communication problem.
2. Show experimental capability and original thinking through verified concept projects.
3. Explain the actual process and deliverables.
4. Present transparently priced starting offers.
5. Enable a straightforward conversation and truthful contact result.

**Approved headline direction:** “PROBLEMS. MEET SOLUTIONS.” This is an editorial direction, not a factual guarantee. Suggested supporting idea: “I design and build distinctive websites that help businesses explain what they do and make the next step clear.” Refine phrasing for originality, accuracy and Malaysian commercial understanding. Do not assert "better conversion", paid-client outcomes, metrics or SEO results absent evidence.

No portrait, fake studio team, invented company registration, fabricated clients, awards, testimonials, years of experience, age or address.
