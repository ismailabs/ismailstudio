# Messaging, copy, and truthful claim guardrails

The user explicitly approved a full rewrite of *creative copy*. This document gives copy direction; it does not claim that speculative headlines are objectively superior or that business scope changed.

## Copy narrative: independently skilled, problem-solving, useful

- Hero headline target: **PROBLEMS. MEET SOLUTIONS.**
- Supporting copy draft: **“I design and build distinctive websites that help businesses make their offer clear and give people a straightforward next step.”**
- Work headline: **“Four ideas. Four directions.”** or expressive alternative, followed by conspicuous **“Concept projects — not commissioned client work.”**
- About: **“Independent by choice. Driven by the problem.”** Explain direct developer access, creative design, sensible scope, code quality; do not imply a team.
- Services: **“A clear starting point. A website shaped around your needs.”** Show exact prices, scope, revisions and exclusions.
- Contact: **“Tell me what needs solving.”** Follow with straightforward request for existing fields.

The old V1 lines may be replaced if more resonant and precise wording is found. Avoid generic "next-level", "unleash", "world-class", "guaranteed", "transform your business", "100% success", "award-winning", "trusted by hundreds" or explicit client success/results not evidenced. The design mockups sometimes include shortened prices, speculative headlines and tiny labels: source data wins.

## FAQ — source-faithful answers

1. **Can I update my website later?** Updates can be arranged through manual code changes or a separately agreed maintenance service; a CMS is not included.
2. **Are domain and hosting included?** Domain registration and recurring third-party costs are separate unless the written quote states otherwise; hosting setup can be discussed.
3. **Will my website appear on Google?** Basic on-page SEO preparation is included; indexing/ranking are decided by search engines and aren't guaranteed.
4. **Do you build online stores or booking systems?** Current packages focus on frontend; checkout, databases and operational booking systems are excluded.
5. **How many revisions are included?** Starter 1; Business/Premium 2, within agreed scope.
6. **What do I need to provide?** Usually business details, services, content/brand assets and feedback, clarified before contracting.
