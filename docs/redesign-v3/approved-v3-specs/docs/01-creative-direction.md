# Creative direction — "BUILT FOR BIGGER PROBLEMS."

## Central statement

Ismail Studio is an ambitious solo frontend development practice based in Melaka. It creates distinctive, useful websites that solve business communication, credibility, discoverability and customer-action problems. The website itself must prove taste and implementation competence without fake metrics.

## Editorial hierarchy

- **First screen:** The very large hero headline, a single high-quality cinematic visual, an immediately clear service proposition, and one obvious enquiry CTA.
- **Second screen:** Shift into a wide, calm **light** work section. Real project screenshots become the focal content.
- **Middle:** Offers as clean, well-spaced light cards with meaningful feature differences; explain scope without walls of text. Market price context must be transparent and sourced.
- **Supporting:** Approach as large Lucide pictograms + short labels and single-sentence guidance; About with real motivation and no fabricated qualifications; FAQ concise and scannable.
- **Closing:** Contact flow as a polished high-contrast section and uncluttered footer.

## Desired quality

Understated, precise, calm, future-forward. Big visuals and assertive typography **rather than** lots of text, tiny labels, excessive glow, decoration, faux analytics metrics, endless micro-cards or manufactured agency buzzwords. The cinematic visual is the climax, not a repeated background on every section.

## Inspiration translation (not replication)

- **OpenAI:** Clear structure, focus and contemporary restraint.
- **Apple:** Large product/visual presentation, clean typography, generous spacing, high-fidelity assets.
- **Anthropic:** Calm editorial pacing and legibility.
- **User cyberpunk images:** Dark atmospheric high-impact hero and luminous iridescent dimensional centerpiece.

These are design principles inferred from the user's requested brands and supplied images, not claims about exact live homepage code/design. Do not copy proprietary logos, images or layouts.

## Do / do not

DO: readable type, generous whitespace, prominent real portfolio projects, big brand mark, reduced-motion alternatives, clean light sections, honest offers.
DON'T: site-wide dark surfaces, orange, minuscule labels, microcopy clutter, glowing card borders everywhere, parallax everywhere, generic glass 3D cube, stats like "real results" or "100% customer success", unverified 24h reply promises.
