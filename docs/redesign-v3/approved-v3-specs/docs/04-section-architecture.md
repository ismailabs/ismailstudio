# V3 page architecture — concise, high-impact, client-focused

The V2 Astro website is the implementation starting point. Keep semantic single-page anchors unless separate project study routes already serve discoverability; preserve working project study URLs where verified. Do not destroy old URLs or canonical behavior without a redirect plan.

## 1. Header / Navigation

New large brand icon+wordmark; slim initial transparent state aligned with hero, transforms into **floating translucent pill** on scroll. Main links Work, Offers/Services, Approach/Process, About, Contact. Primary CTA Get a Website Quote (not an invented response time). Visible focus and keyboard support. Active section may be indicated subtly; no disappearing navigation.

## 2. Cinematic hero (dark)

H1: **BUILT FOR BIGGER PROBLEMS.** One or two lines of supporting positioning maximum, e.g. 'Websites built to make your business clearer, more credible and easier to find.' (draft; avoid guarantees). CTA1: Request a website quote; CTA2: Explore work. Cinematic image/iridescent art occupies a substantial zone (~45–60%) without obscuring H1. Melaka, Malaysia and national focus nearby but not repeated everywhere. No fabricated hero metrics, no personal age.

## 3. Selected work (LIGHT)

Immediately transitions to a mostly white section with a large original heading, four genuine project screenshots with generous visual dimensions and concise labels. Could use a two-column featured gallery desktop and stacked full-width cards on mobile; no tiny cards. Each opens an accessible project study view and offers a genuine 'View live concept' link. Disclose self-initiated concepts visibly.

## 4. Problem / solution editorial statement (LIGHT)

One large sentence and at most four clear themes: clarity, credibility, discoverability, action. Pair one tasteful visual artifact or diagrams if needed. Avoid overexplaining agency-style values, invented conversion numbers or fake 'before/after' outcomes.

## 5. Three offers (LIGHT)

Large differentiated rounded cards with names, customer situation, starting price and scope, tiered SEO highlights and enquiry actions. Premium should not appear more expensive solely because it contains filler features; make clear what changes. No 'Most Popular' claim absent real evidence. Detailed terms are accessible, not crammed into the hero.

## 6. Market comparison (LIGHT neutral)

A restrained strip or modest data display beneath packages. Show only dated sourced published ranges / comparably scoped offers and methodology. Never imply every competitor is costlier. Omit if source quality or comparability fails.

## 7. Process / Approach (LIGHT)

Replace numbered steps with large, obvious **Lucide icons** + short labels such as Discover, Define, Design & Build, Review, Launch. Keep at least a brief accessible text cue per step and coherent order. Examples: messages-square, clipboard-list, code-2, scan-eye, rocket. Final icon mapping should be reviewed, not treated as exact official names until library verified.

## 8. About (optional dark contrasting block)

Ambition, problem-solving approach and direct communication. No fake office building, fake portrait, age, fabricated experience statistics or unverified claims. Use a genuine distinctive brand symbol at substantial size as supporting visual; Melaka location accurate.

## 9. FAQ (LIGHT)

Six concise questions with direct answers: What is included? Who owns the website and domain? How long does a project take (variable, no false fixed promise)? What does SEO include and not guarantee? What happens after launch? Can you help with edits/maintenance? Reflect actual contractual and provider boundaries; answer uncertainties honestly.

## 10. Guided contact (contrast with main light narrative)

Existing Formspree-to-professional-mail integration retained. Three step UI: identity, project needs, review/send. Keep field names/payload semantics, help messages, required validation, spam honeypot, native POST fallback, email fallback, focus handling and browser history behavior. No extra WhatsApp field or response SLA unless explicitly verified/authorized.

## 11. Footer

Strong original logo, clear contact CTA, links, truthful location and copyright. Short—not another wall of navigation or tiny legal lines.
