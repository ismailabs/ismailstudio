## Carefully bounded SEO examples (proposed package scopes, NOT contractual guarantees)

**Starter / Basic On-Page:** One-page semantic heading hierarchy, clear descriptive links, page title and meta description, relevant alternative text, readability and basic search-friendly markup.

**Business / Technical:** Starter foundation across included pages plus one-time checks and remedies for canonical URLs, sitemap/robots configuration where applicable, crawl/indexing directive issues, responsive behavior and performance fundamentals, with a brief technical handover. Do not advertise Google Search Console account setup unless the customer owns and authorizes account access.

**Premium / Advanced On-Site:** Business foundation plus focused one-time keyword/search-intent mapping, content and metadata alignment for agreed pages, appropriate and truthful structured data where eligible, internal-linking review, technical/on-site audit and concise prioritized recommendations. Specific keyword quantities, separate copywriting, backlinks, offsite SEO, ongoing content work, monitoring, ranking guarantees and Search Console ownership are excluded unless individually quoted.

## Publishing guardrails

- All three packages receive solid technical quality as ordinary website-engineering hygiene; differentiators are **additional documented work**, not permission to make basic sites broken or inaccessible.
- No promised results ('rank #1', 'guaranteed leads', 'SEO boosts traffic 3×'). Google states crawl/indexing/ranking aren't guaranteed: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
- No claim 'most popular' without actual verified sales data. Visually highlight Business only as 'Good for a growing site' or neutral design emphasis.
- No guaranteed turnaround or support duration without actual agreed policy.
- Package details should be concise on cards, expanded in accessible disclosure/link before enquiry. Final agreement/quote defines exact work, copy and revision boundaries.
