# Copywriting rules and explicit generated-image corrections

## Approved writing style

Short, direct, clear, optimistic about building useful things; personal ambition without inflated identity or agency pretending. Emphasize clarity, credibility, discoverability and easier customer action. Use simple English appropriate for Malaysian customers. Keep most descriptions to one or two sentences. Strong headlines should carry the storytelling load.

## Primary H1

**BUILT FOR BIGGER PROBLEMS.** (Approved.)

Suggested supporting sentence (draft, not locked copy): 'I build websites that make businesses easier to understand, trust and contact.'

## Claims explicitly NOT approved by visual mockups

- 'Real Businesses. Real Results.' — four works are self-initiated **concept projects**, not paid client results.
- '100% independent', '100% customer satisfaction', 'guaranteed growth', 'measurable impact', 'SEO ranking improvement' — do not replace evidence with percentages.
- 'Most Popular' package — no independent sales data.
- 'Get back within 24 hours', 'fast response within 1–2 days', fixed 2–6 week turnaround — not verified commitments.
- New WhatsApp enquiry field, public personal phone number, official registration/company location outside Melaka — do not add.
- Authored project photography in generated images — does not show actual live demo pages.
- Paid case-study claims ('client asked...', 'we increased bookings...', 'ecommerce conversion lift...').
- Literal market ranges or comparison claims embedded in generative designs.
- "Based in Malaysia, world-ready" international-service claims unless explicitly authorized. Public location wording should be **'Based in Melaka, serving businesses across Malaysia'**.
- Exact logo vector shape is not supplied. Treat original new symbol as logo brief and reference, not completed trademark.

## Preferred sections copy intent

Hero: problem-solving, frontend websites, location, single strong CTA.
Work: 'Four self-initiated website concepts' with truthful labels and names.
Offers: what the customer needs, what's included, SEO distinction, scope limitations.
Process: five short action verbs + one concrete explanation each.
About: ambitious problem-solver, hands-on frontend craft, direct relationship.
FAQ: practical scope and risks, concise and truthful.
Contact: simple enquiry, transparent handling, no invented SLA.

## Privacy

Do not put age, home address, personal number or private emails on the site. Professional email `contact@ismailstudio.my` only. No personally identifying data should be included in demo screenshots or repository test fixtures. All form success UI must reflect actual provider acceptance, not a visual animation alone.
