# Visual authority — four images, one coherent design

**Primary image:** `approved-v3-specs/assets/approved-mockups/01-hero-built-for-bigger-problems.png` — signature dark hero and H1. Other three: `02-work-case-study-homepage.png`, `03-light-offers-market-comparison.png`, `04-mobile-dark-light-layout.png`. All are mandatory to review at full resolution. `approved-v3-specs/boards/approved-visual-map.jpg` is only an index.

## Visual invariants (must appear in rendered browser)

1. **Hero:** dominant large uppercase **BUILT FOR BIGGER PROBLEMS.**, cinematic mountain depth, original iridescent sculpture/glass art, clear distinct service sentence, generous primary quote CTA; no old [IS] cube; visible major mark/wordmark.
2. **Transition:** dark atmospheric hero leads deliberately into a **bright, white/off-white large Work section** with major authentic project screenshots and generous image space.
3. **Body:** mostly bright sections with clear typographic hierarchy and plenty of negative space; no endless dark panels, tiny text, decorative hairlines/eyebrow clutter or high-density agency cards.
4. **Logo:** minimalist black/white round-organic symbol and crisp lowercase Ismail Studio wordmark at usable sizes; vector recreated originally, not a PNG slice.
5. **Offers:** three refined light cards, large prices, differentiated one-time SEO, meaningful icons, no 'Most Popular' without data. Market evidence is restrained and factual.
6. **Mobile:** text naturally reflows, artwork crops intelligently, work cards are large and distinctly authentic, menus/dialogs/prices legible, no repeated background picture.
7. **Motion:** compact translucent floating nav after scroll, purposeful low-amplitude animation, no WebGL requirement, no motion that obstructs reading or form.

## Approved art not approved copy

Generated images include fake project imagery, 'Real Businesses/Real Results', invented client results, unsourced 24h replies, auto-created testimonials, generated ranges and inconsistent page counts/SEO. Ignore all of these as facts. The official typed data, V3 corrected specs and validated project assets control. Do not transplant imagery from generated example project cards to the site.

## Art composition strategy

Use a properly cropped optimized cinematic *hero background layer* and carefully composed iridescent focal overlay, with legible gradient/scrim zone behind actual H1. A single hero renders at desktop/mobile by responsive art direction, not by faking pages with the whole raster screenshot. Public background-study crop is available; final art should have clean production rights, performance review and original symbol differentiation. Chrome bling must not recur as decorative clutter down the page.

Avoid exact pixel-match tests against imperfect generative text or thumbnails; judge composition, silhouette, scale, spacing, contrast, responsive intent and business truth. Record every deliberate deviation from reference with screenshot.
