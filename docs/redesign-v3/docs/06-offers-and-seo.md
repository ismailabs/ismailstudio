# Three website offers — binding commercial facts and transparent SEO

| Offer | Price displayed | Pages | Revision rounds | One-time SEO tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | **From RM599** | **1 page** | **1** | Basic On-Page SEO |
| Business | **From RM1,299** | **Up to 5 pages** | **2** | Technical SEO setup |
| Premium | **From RM2,999** | **Up to 12 pages** | **2** | Advanced On-Site SEO setup/audit |

Package names, starting amounts and limits are immutable absent new explicit user instruction. These are **frontend website projects**, not monthly SEO subscriptions or full backend packages. No automatic hosting/domain/maintenance/payment/operational booking inclusion. Scope confirmed in written quote; no promise of ranked traffic or number-one position. Business may be visually prominent but cannot say 'Most Popular' without sales evidence.

## Defined scope proposal (one-time, proportionate)

- **Starter Basic On-Page:** title/meta descriptions, semantic headings, descriptive links, alt text for meaningful imagery, readable content and foundational HTML SEO for included one page.
- **Business Technical:** basic quality for included pages + checks/remediation of canonical host, sitemap, robots directives, search-accessible static content, valid links, mobile/reflow and diagnosed speed basics. 'Technical' describes *additional documented checks*, not permission to skip basic SEO on Starter.
- **Premium Advanced On-Site:** Business foundation + focused keyword/search-intent mapping and on-site content alignment for agreed pages, eligible truthful structured data, internal-link review, technical findings with prioritized recommendations. **No fixed keyword count, copywriting package, external backlinks, recurring audit, ranking guarantee or Google account access implied.**

Read the full prior source document `approved-v3-specs/docs/08-offers-seo-scope.md`; it takes precedence over mockup-invented copy. Before release, check whether every stated feature can be delivered at starting prices without hidden deliverables; if not, narrow copy truthfully rather than quietly increasing mandatory prices.

## Card microcopy (candidate, editable)

Starter: *Get the essentials right.* Business: *Make more information easy to find.* Premium: *Build a broader site with stronger on-site search foundations.* CTA should prefill matching selected package. Use simple well-spaced comparison before the technical footnote, avoid marketing buzzword piles.
