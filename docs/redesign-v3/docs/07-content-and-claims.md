# Copywriting and truth register

## Tone

Ambitious yet grounded; confident but not self-congratulatory. Clear Malaysian business English, short lines/sentences, strong typographic headlines with minimal secondary text. Avoid hollow slogans like 'world class' without proof.

**Fixed primary H1:** `BUILT FOR BIGGER PROBLEMS.`. H1 is creative framing, followed immediately by a straightforward accurate sentence that Ismail Studio designs/builds frontend websites for businesses from Melaka across Malaysia. Suggested alternative (not mandatory): *I build clear, distinctive websites that help businesses explain what they do and make it easy to enquire.*

**Location:** 'Based in Melaka, serving businesses across Malaysia.' Do not incorrectly claim based only in Malaysia or 'world ready' international service, age, private street address or fabricated studio office. 'Ambitious' replaces 'independent' as headline positioning; independence is factual as solo practice but need not dominate public messaging.

**Proof:** Four named owned live **Concept Projects**, accurately labelled. No commissioned clients, revenue, leads, rankings, conversion rates, guarantees, badges, stock-looking customer photos, quotes or fake case-study outcomes. Actual project case-study intent should distinguish scenario vs demonstrated implementation.

**Form:** `contact@ismailstudio.my`, existing fields and privacy notice; no personal WhatsApp, no reply within 24h promise, no unsupported fixed project timetable. Preserve provider errors honestly.

**Package:** Display 'From' in all 3, correct pages/revisions, one-time SEO and explicit exclusions. No 'Most Popular', 'free maintenance', 'unlimited revisions', SEO ranking claims, operational booking/e-commerce platform or recurring support unless later independently contracted.

**Competitive value:** No 'cheaper than all agencies'. When the published evidence is weak, remove price chart and provide an honest transparent-scope explanation. Distinguish concrete publicly verified provider prices and factual methodology from inference.
