Checked 2026-10-22, Malaysia date. Three Malaysian providers' own live pricing pages were opened; search snippets, general price guides, adverts and aggregators were not used as authority. This is a small convenience sample, not a national average or a matched competitive basket.

No checkout, enquiry, contact, signup or other action was performed on these sites. No third-party phone number or private information is copied into the product.

## Decision

The evidence supports only the particular dated examples above. It cannot support the mockup's freelancer/agency market ranges, claims of universal savings, an industry average or like-for-like value ranking. Page count alone does not make scope comparable: CMS, integrations, hosting, content and care differ, and some recurring terms need clarification.

The handoff permits omitting numeric comparisons when comparability is inadequate. The public site therefore uses the restrained scope-context panel below its three offers. It states included responsive layouts, design approach, one-time SEO and separately scoped costs, and makes no external pricing or “cheapest” claim. The three Ismail Studio starting amounts remain RM599, RM1,299 and RM2,999. Adding a future numeric comparison requires release-day rechecking, clear units and costs, and a caveated list of examples rather than an invented market range.
