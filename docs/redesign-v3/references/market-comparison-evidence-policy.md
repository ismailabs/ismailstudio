# Malaysian pricing research protocol — no invented comparison charts

Owner approves evidence-based Malaysian published price RANGES only, with exact citations/dates/scope notes. A `market average` must not be asserted unless data are representative, well specified, and methodology supports it. A few marketing blog entries are not a national survey.

Before choosing on-page comparison numbers:

1. Research multiple recent public sources for Malaysian one-page / multi-page business websites, prioritizing agency sites with explicit MYR currency, date and deliverables. Include useful budget and business-site ranges, not unrelated enterprise platforms.
2. Make apples-to-apples columns: page band, responsive implementation, whether hosting/domain/copy/backend/SEO/revisions include taxes/fees and updates. Refuse invalid equivalence.
3. Record a CSV/JSON source register with URL, source owner, captured date, published/updated date, quoted amount, currency, included work, exclusions, archive/screenshot path, evidence grade.
4. Cite immediately adjacent to any published numeric range in user-visible HTML, and link a transparent methodology/more details. Label them "published indicative ranges", not audited statistical averages.
5. Display Ismail Studio's real exact STARTING prices with scope and avoid the unsupported claim 'cheaper than everyone.' If suitable comparables insufficient/stale, replace the entire numeric comparison with a neutral "Why pricing varies" visual.
6. Recheck broken/stale links before release. Do not hardcode 2024–2025 figures copied from AI visuals, pretend SEO extras are included by competitors, or invent price sources to fill empty cards.

External reference candidates are research starting points, not accepted statistics or content to duplicate. This handoff purposefully leaves competitor amounts out of its machine assertion manifest until grounded research is done in the Codex environment.
